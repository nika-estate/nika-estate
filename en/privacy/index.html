Privacy Notice

Version dated 28 September 2026

1. Scope and controller

This Notice explains how enquiries submitted through the English-language Nika Estate landing pages on this site are handled. These pages include property selection questionnaires, guide requests and consultation requests. It does not replace the separate notice for the weekly video digest on other Nika Estate websites.

The controller for these English-language enquiries is NIKA ESTATE HOMES PROPERTIES, trading as Nika Estate, with a registered address at Office Nos. AA2/504, AA2/505 and AA2/507, Mankhool, Bur Dubai, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Privacy contact: info.dubai@nikaestate.com.

2. Information we collect

The exact fields depend on the form. They may include your name, phone number, email address if requested, preferred contact channel, Telegram username if you provide one, and your answers about your property goal, preferred country or location, budget, purchase approach and other preferences. We may also record the page and form used, submission time, referral and UTM information, request identifier, and confirmation that you selected the consent checkbox. Technical logs and analytics services may receive browser information, cookies and IP address. These forms do not request passport, payment-card, biometric or sensitive personal data.

3. Why we use the information

  • to receive and answer your enquiry through the contact channel you chose;
  • to prepare the guide, property shortlist, calculation or consultation you requested;
  • to understand your preferences and avoid duplicate responses;
  • to maintain the forms, protect them from misuse and measure campaign performance;
  • to respond to privacy requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Email campaigns, calls or messages unrelated to your request require a separate lawful basis or separate consent.

4. Basis and processing

For an enquiry, we rely on the consent you provide by selecting a separate, unticked checkbox before submitting the form. We may collect, record, organise, store, update, retrieve, use, disclose to authorised service providers, restrict, delete and otherwise process the information for the stated purposes. Some of these actions are automated. The corresponding Data Processing Consent is available separately.

5. Service providers and other platforms

Authorised Nika Estate staff and providers supporting enquiry handling may access information only as needed for their work. The current forms send submissions through Google Apps Script to a working Google Sheet. Static pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. If you choose Telegram or another messenger, that platform handles your communications under its own terms and privacy notice.

Some pages use Meta Pixel and Yandex Metrica for analytics. They receive page-view and confirmed-enquiry events; our form code does not intentionally send your name, phone number or email address to those analytics services. Providers may process information outside your country of residence. We handle any international transfers in accordance with applicable requirements.

6. Retention and security

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to answer your request, provide the requested material and complete agreed follow-up, or until you withdraw consent, unless applicable law requires a longer period. Access is limited by work responsibilities, and we use organisational and technical safeguards. When the purpose ends, we delete or anonymise the information unless we have a lawful reason to retain it.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask for access, correction, restriction or deletion of your information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info.dubai@nikaestate.com. Please identify the contact details used for your enquiry so we can locate it. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before we received it. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Other websites and messenger platforms have their own privacy notices. Updates to this Notice will appear on this page with a new version date.
