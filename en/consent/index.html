By completing an enquiry form on an English-language Nika Estate landing page, selecting the separate consent checkbox and submitting the form, I freely consent to NIKA ESTATE HOMES PROPERTIES, trading as Nika Estate, processing my personal information as described below. Its registered address is Office Nos. AA2/504, AA2/505 and AA2/507, Mankhool, Bur Dubai, Dubai, United Arab Emirates; its privacy email is info.dubai@nikaestate.com.

1. Purposes

To receive and respond to my enquiry; contact me through the channel I selected; prepare and provide the guide, property shortlist, calculation or consultation I requested; take account of my property preferences; prevent duplicate enquiries; and administer the forms and related communications.

2. Information covered

This consent covers the information I provide in the particular form: my name, phone number, email address if requested, Telegram username and preferred contact channel if provided, answers about my property goal, preferred market, budget and purchase approach, and any enquiry text. It also covers the submission date and time, page and referral information, UTM parameters, request identifier and record of my consent. Not every form requests every item.

3. Processing and recipients

I consent to the collection, recording, organisation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, disclosure to authorised Nika Estate staff and service providers, restriction and deletion of this information, using automated or manual means where appropriate. The current enquiry route may use Google Apps Script and a working Google Sheet. If I choose to contact Nika Estate in Telegram or another messenger, that platform also handles the communication under its own rules.

4. Duration and withdrawal

This consent takes effect when I submit the form and lasts until the purposes are fulfilled or I withdraw it, unless applicable law permits or requires longer retention. I may withdraw it by emailing info.dubai@nikaestate.com and identifying the contact details used in the form. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out before it was received.

This consent is for responding to my enquiry and providing what I requested. It is not consent to unrelated marketing emails, calls or messenger campaigns.
