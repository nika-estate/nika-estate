Property buyer’s starter guide

A practical first pass for overseas buyers: what to verify before reserving a property, how to think about the full budget, and which questions to ask about future rental income.

Start with eligibility, not a property ad.

Saudi Arabia's updated non-Saudi ownership framework took effect on 22 January 2026. Eligibility depends on your buyer category, the property location and the rights available in that zone. The official Saudi Properties portal is the place to verify the applicable route. A property purchase does not automatically grant citizenship or residency.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Check your route

  • Are you a Saudi resident, a non-resident individual or buying through a company?
  • Is the exact property within a permitted geographical zone for your category?
  • What rights can you acquire, and for how long?
  • Which documents and registrations are needed before transfer?
02 / TOTAL BUDGET

Count every cost

  • Purchase price and payment schedule.
  • Applicable taxes, registration and transaction charges.
  • Furnishing, fit-out and any mortgage costs.
  • Annual service, insurance, management and maintenance expenses.
Total entry budget = price + transaction costs + setup costs + cash reserve.
03 / PROPERTY CHECK

Review the actual unit

  • Seller and developer identity, title and project approvals.
  • Unit size, permitted use, handover scope and delivery terms.
  • Contract terms for delays, changes, resale and cancellation.
  • Who manages the building and what the service agreement covers.
04 / RENTAL PLAN

Model net income

  • Use comparable signed rents, not only asking prices.
  • Allow for vacancy, management, repairs and service charges.
  • Check whether the unit's use and letting model are permitted.
  • Stress-test a lower-rent and longer-vacancy scenario.
Illustrative net cash flow = collected rent − operating costs − financing costs.

Planning to buy remotely? Ask which identity, authorization, signing and registration steps can be completed remotely in your specific case. Do not assume every stage is remote.

05 / YOUR CONSULTATION

What to prepare for a useful conversation

Share your intended use, approximate budget, preferred city, purchase timing and whether an in-person visit is possible. Nika Estate can use this to narrow down properties and identify the conditions that need confirmation before you make a decision.
