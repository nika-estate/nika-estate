NIKA ESTATE · GREECE

Property buyer’s starter guide

A first-pass checklist for buyers comparing a Greek home, an investment and an investor residence permit.

Property choice and residence route go together.

Greece has an investor residence permit often called the Golden Visa. Eligibility depends on the current rules, the applicant and the qualifying investment. Confirm the property category and permit conditions before making a reservation.

01 / LOCATION

Start with your life plan

  • Compare Athens, coastal areas and islands against your purpose.
  • Consider year-round access, services and property management.
  • Visit the neighbourhood where possible.
02 / RESIDENCE ROUTE

Check the current criteria

  • Ask which investor permit category may apply.
  • Confirm current property requirements and supporting documents.
  • Check family-member and renewal conditions before buying.
03 / PROPERTY CHECK

Review title and costs

  • Ask independent counsel to examine title and encumbrances.
  • Check the contract, permitted use and building documentation.
  • Budget for taxes, notary, registration, repairs and annual expenses.
04 / RENTAL PLAN

Use conservative assumptions

  • Verify the legal conditions for your proposed rental model.
  • Model vacancy, management, maintenance and taxes.
  • Do not treat advertised gross yield as net income.

A residence permit is decided through the official procedure. Property purchase alone does not guarantee approval or future rental income.

05 / YOUR CONSULTATION

What to prepare

Share your goal, approximate budget, preferred area, timing and whether you can visit. Nika Estate can use this to narrow the options and identify conditions to verify before a decision.

Official starting points: Hellenic Ministry of Migration and Asylum — Golden Visa. Recheck current rules and individual eligibility before reserving. This guide is educational and does not promise a legal status, approval or investment return.
