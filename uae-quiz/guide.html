Property buyer’s starter guide

A first-pass checklist for buyers comparing Dubai and Abu Dhabi: what to ask before reserving, what belongs in the full budget, and how to test a rental plan.

Choose the market and property rights first.

Dubai and Abu Dhabi have different registries and procedures. Verify the title, permitted ownership rights and registration route for the exact unit. A property purchase does not automatically produce a residence approval or a particular rental return.

01 / LOCATION

Compare Dubai and Abu Dhabi

  • Where would you actually want to live or manage a rental?
  • What transport, work and daily services matter to you?
  • Compare units and local demand within the same property category.
02 / FULL BUDGET

Count the real entry cost

  • Purchase price and payment schedule.
  • Registration, brokerage, financing and handover charges applicable to the transaction.
  • Furnishing, service charges, insurance and a cash reserve.
03 / PROPERTY CHECK

Verify the specific unit

  • Seller or developer, title and project registration.
  • Handover scope, service charges and contract terms.
  • Whether the intended use and letting plan are permitted.
04 / RENTAL PLAN

Model net cash flow

  • Use comparable achieved rents rather than only advertised rents.
  • Allow for vacancy, management, maintenance and service charges.
  • Stress-test a lower-rent scenario.

If residency is part of your goal, check the current investor category and evidence requirements with the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship (ICP) before treating it as part of a purchase decision.

05 / YOUR CONSULTATION

What to prepare
