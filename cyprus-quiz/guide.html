Property buyer’s starter guide

A practical checklist for buyers considering life, a second home or an investment in the Republic of Cyprus.

Start with your reason for moving.

A home, rental investment and immigration plan can point to different properties. For non-EU buyers, property acquisition permission and an investor immigration permit are separate questions. Confirm eligibility and current criteria before committing to a unit.

01 / YOUR LOCATION

Choose the right city

  • Compare your daily life in Limassol, Paphos and Larnaca.
  • Check schools, healthcare, airports and year-round services.
  • Visit the neighbourhood at different times if possible.
02 / LEGAL ROUTE

Separate purchase and status

  • Check whether permission to acquire property is required for your citizenship.
  • Ask which documents and conditions apply to an investor immigration permit.
  • Confirm family-member rules and ongoing conditions with an adviser.
03 / PROPERTY CHECK

Review the actual unit

  • Title, planning permits and any contract of sale filing.
  • Handover specification, common expenses and maintenance.
  • The full payment schedule, tax position and transaction costs.
04 / RENTAL PLAN

Plan beyond gross rent

  • Check whether the intended letting model is allowed.
  • Allow for vacancy, management and repairs.
  • Test net cash flow with conservative occupancy assumptions.

An immigration permit is subject to official criteria and an individual decision. Buying a property alone does not guarantee approval.

05 / YOUR CONSULTATION

What to prepare

Share your goal, approximate budget, preferred area, timing and whether you can visit. Nika Estate can use this to narrow the options and identify conditions to verify before a decision.

Official starting points: Cyprus Ministry of Interior — Purchasing Property · Cyprus Migration Department — Immigration Permits for Investors. Recheck current rules and individual eligibility before reserving. This guide is educational and does not promise a legal status, approval or investment return.
